Settings

Appearance

Place the finder beside the page when checked. Otherwise it overlays the page. The collapsed bar always has its own space.

Scanning

Background checks listings without opening tabs. Visible tabs opens one scan tab and reuses it for every listing. Mixed reuses a visible tab only when a background check fails, then returns to background scanning. Visible tabs and Mixed check one listing at a time. Press Escape to stop. Applies when you next click Scan.

Default: 1 week. Saved listings survive browser restarts. Age is measured from retrieval, not reuse; 1 month means 30 days. Opening a listing refreshes its saved data.

Background defaults to 5. Visible tabs and Mixed show 1; switching back restores your Background value. Changes apply to the next scan.

More than 20 can trigger eBay request limits or verification checks.
