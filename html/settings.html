Settings

Appearance

Place the finder beside the page when checked. Otherwise it overlays the page. The collapsed bar always has its own space.

Scanning

Background checks listings without opening tabs. Visible tabs opens one scan tab and reuses it for every listing. Mixed starts with your chosen parallel count. On an error it pauses new background requests, lets active requests finish, and checks failed listings one at a time in the visible tab. It then resumes parallel background scanning. Visible tabs always checks one listing at a time. Press Escape to stop. Applies when you next click Scan.

Default: 1 week. Saved listings survive browser restarts. Age is measured from retrieval, not reuse; 1 month means 30 days. Opening a listing refreshes its saved data.

Background and Mixed share your saved parallel count (default 5). Visible tabs shows 1; switching back restores your saved value. Changes apply to the next scan.

More than 20 can trigger eBay request limits or verification checks.
