Appearance
Resize eBay

When checked, the finder sits beside the page. Otherwise it overlays the content.

The collapsed bar always has its own space.

Scanning
Scanning modes
  • Background: checks listings without opening tabs.
  • Visible tabs: opens one tab and reuses it, checking one listing at a time.
  • Mixed: starts with your parallel count. On an error, new requests pause while active requests finish. Failed listings are checked one at a time in the visible tab, then parallel background scanning resumes.

Press Escape to stop. Changes apply when you next click Scan.

Saved listing age

Default: 1 week. Saved listings survive browser restarts.

Age starts at retrieval, not reuse. One month means 30 days.

Opening a listing refreshes its saved data.

Listings in parallel

Background and Mixed share your saved count (default 5).

Visible tabs shows 1. Switching back restores your saved value.

Changes apply to the next scan.

High parallel count: more than 20 can trigger eBay request limits or verification checks.

Storage
Always show all cached data

Off by default. When enabled, opening the finder on any search results page loads all unexpired cached listings for that eBay marketplace, even from other queries and results pages.

This reads saved data only: no scan starts and no listings are fetched. Your Variant and Listing filters, sorting and hidden-list choices still apply.

Cached prices and stock are not live. Opening a listing or using Refresh retrieves fresh data. Retention and Purge all cache still apply.

Local storage used
Local storage usage

Includes saved listings, search snapshots and settings. The size updates as storage changes.

Purge all cache removes cached listing data and scan snapshots. Your preferences, hidden-list choices and remembered missing listings are kept.

Scans and opened listings can save fresh data afterwards.

Missing listings
Remembered missing listings

Listings confirmed missing on eBay are remembered indefinitely and skipped in future scans, including ghost search results.

Clear remembered missing listings forgets all of these records, allowing future scans to try those IDs again. Your cached prices, preferences and hidden-list choices are kept.

If you manually open a remembered listing and it loads successfully, its fresh data automatically replaces the missing record. Cache retention and Purge all cache do not erase missing records.
